Chain Pop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Chain Pop ("the app") is published by Matada Construction s.r.o., Záhumenní 2034/103, Poruba, 708 00 Ostrava, company ID (IČO) 19818106 ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email or contacts, and the app has no user accounts or servers of ours. Your game progress (level, coins, best score, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, IDFA, if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location, and information about how you interact with ads. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. More about how Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google may process this data outside the European Economic Area, as described in its policy.

On iOS you decide whether the app may track you through the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. Consent is the legal basis for personalized ads. You can play without agreeing and will then see non-personalized ads. You can withdraw your consent at any time by turning off tracking in iOS Settings, by reinstalling the app (which asks again), or by contacting us.

Game Center

The app can use Apple Game Center. Signing in is optional. If you are signed in, the app sends your best score and best chain to Game Center, and other players can see them on the leaderboard together with your Game Center name. This data is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive or store it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

We do not store personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. Deleting the app removes all game data from your device. For data processed by Google or Apple, you can exercise your rights (access, deletion, objection, withdrawal of consent) with them under their privacy policies above. If you are in the EU, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in the Czech Republic this is the Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).
