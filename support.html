Frequently asked questions

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your level, coins, unlocks and settings are stored on your device. They are not synced to an account, so deleting the app or switching phones without restoring a device backup will reset your progress.

How do I change ad tracking?

Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your iPhone and turn tracking off for Chain Pop. See our Privacy Policy for details.

The world ranking does not open.

The world ranking uses Apple Game Center. Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in Settings › Game Center on your iPhone. Playing the game does not require signing in.

A rewarded video did not give me my reward.

Watch the video until it finishes and close it with the close button. If no ad is available, try again in a moment. If you still did not receive the reward, contact us.
